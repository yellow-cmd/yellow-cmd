yellow-cmd backup

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

yellow-cmd backup ("the app") is a personal backup tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. This policy explains what the app does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) to upload, list, move, download and delete the owner's backup files. It does not access any other Google data or any other person's data.

How the data is used

Only to back up the owner's files to the owner's Drive and to restore them. The app has no servers: it runs on the owner's computer and talks directly to Google Drive.

Storage

The OAuth sign-in token is stored only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's computer. Backed-up files are stored only in the owner's Google Drive.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, analytics or to train AI models, and no person other than the owner reads it.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Backups stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them; files deleted locally are removed from the backup after 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deleting the local rclone configuration removes the stored token.

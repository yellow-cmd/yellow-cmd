yellow-cmd backup

A personal tool, not a public service.

yellow-cmd backup is a private, single-user backup setup. Every night it copies folders from the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive, and lets the owner restore them. It runs on the open-source rclone, signed in with the owner's own Google account.

What it does with Google Drive

  • Uploads the owner's files to a backup folder in the owner's Drive.
  • Moves files the owner deleted into a dated folder there, and removes them after 30 days.
  • Lists and downloads those files when the owner restores them.

Nobody else uses this app. No data leaves the owner's computer and Google Drive, and nothing is shared, sold or analysed.
